# Fareward Christmas ad copy (Meta / Instagram)

Rules applied: explain the credit first, "many airlines", "airline credits", no promise of a credit, % over $, only real numbers, faceless, fareward.com is the only brand mark. The fee line ("Fareward keeps 20% of the savings.") is on the creative, not in the captions or primary text.
Every ad uses the CTA button **Sign Up** and links to `https://www.fareward.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=christmas_2026&utm_content=<ad-id>`

## Creative notes (v4, Nov 27)
- The +105% price-increase ad and its reel are gone. Every ad leads with potential savings, not increases.
- Locked type system on every frame (feed, story, and reels): headline 96, one support line 44, two-line chip 36, fee 26. Same positions on every ad (feed and story each have a fixed vertical grid). Headlines are written to fit; type is never shrunk.
- Three text levels plus a small footer: headline, "Many airlines give airline credits.", and a cream chip with a real 41% save plus "One real save, not a typical result."
- Color Christmas emojis (Noto Color Emoji) sit in the same spot on every ad, one per frame: A 🎄, B 🎁, C ✈️, D ❄️.
- Cream `#F7F1E4` / white `#FFFFFF` on a dark overlay. The only brand mark is **fareward.com**, bottom-right, in Fareward blue `#2F5D9E`.
- Reels are 6.5s (reels 1 and 2) and 6.8s (reel 3 voiceover bed). Music is Chill Wave by Kevin MacLeod.
