# First Five: DM scripts

## (a) First DM to a commenter
Hey! Thanks for commenting. [Destination] sounds great ✈️

You're in the running for one of our 5 free spots. Here's how to lock it in:

1. Reply with the email address you'll forward from, so we can match it to your handle.
2. Forward your booking confirmation to trips@fareward.com from that email.
3. That's it. The first 5 forwards that match a commenter get Fareward at 0% fee.

We watch your fare until takeoff. If it drops, you keep 100% of the credit. Works with American, Alaska, Hawaiian, Delta, United, and Southwest, one trip per person.

## (b) "You're in" confirmation (forward matched)
You're in! 🎉 We got your confirmation for [trip] and matched it to your account.

You're one of our first 5, so your fee is 0%. We're watching your fare now and will keep watching until takeoff. If it drops, you keep 100% of the credit. We'll message you as soon as one lands.

## (c) All 5 spots taken
Thanks so much for jumping in! All 5 free spots have been claimed.

You can still have us watch your trip: forward your confirmation to trips@fareward.com. It's free to watch, and we only take 20% after a credit lands, so you keep the rest. If nothing drops, you pay nothing.
